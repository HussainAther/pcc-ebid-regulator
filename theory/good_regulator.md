# Conant-Ashby Good Regulator Theorem

## Question

What aspects of a cyclic PCC system must be represented by a regulator for good regulation?

The working idea is not that the theorem fails. Rather, PCC may sharpen what counts as a relevant "model of the system" when behavior is persistently cyclic or non-convergent.

### Hypothesis GRT-1

In a cyclic regime, a regulator supplied with information about interaction topology and/or cycle phase will outperform an otherwise comparable state-only regulator on long-run regulation error.

### Important caution

A result in a toy PCC simulation would demonstrate a property of that model class, not a new proof or extension of the Good Regulator Theorem.
