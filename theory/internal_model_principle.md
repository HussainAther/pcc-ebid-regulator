# Internal Model Principle

The Internal Model Principle motivates a related question: if the dominant disturbance-like behavior is a persistent endogenous cycle, must a robust regulator contain a representation of that cycle's dynamics?

### Hypothesis IMP-1

Controllers that represent the dominant cyclic mode should reject or anticipate PCC-generated variability more effectively than controllers without that representation, when matched for comparable capacity.

The key experimental requirement is to distinguish gains from **model structure** from gains caused merely by adding parameters or memory.
