# Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety

## Classical role

Requisite variety concerns the relationship between the variety of disturbances, the variety available to a regulator, and the variety remaining in outcomes.

This repository does **not** assume that EBID entropy is identical to Ashby's variety. The first computational question is weaker: whether an independently defined PCC/EBID instability measure predicts how much regulator repertoire is empirically required.

## PCC question

In the simplest exogenous-disturbance picture, the regulator acts on a system while the disturbance source is conceptually separate. In PCC, Control participates in a cycle with Pressure and Chaos. Acting as a regulator may therefore alter subsequent disturbance-generating dynamics.

### Hypothesis RV-1

The minimum regulator variety needed for bounded performance increases with PCC instability over at least one nontrivial parameter regime.

### Falsification direction

RV-1 is weakened if regulator variety is unrelated to the selected instability measure after controlling for obvious scale effects, or if the relationship disappears across reasonable model variants.
