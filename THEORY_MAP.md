# Theory Map

| Framework | Primary question | Classical object | PCC/EBID bridge | Initial test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ashby: Requisite Variety | How much regulatory capacity is needed? | disturbance and regulator variety | endogenous instability may make requisite variety trajectory-dependent | sweep action repertoire vs PCC/EBID instability |
| Conant-Ashby: Good Regulator | What must a good regulator represent? | a model of the regulated system | topology/phase may be relevant parts of the model in cyclic systems | state-only vs topology-aware regulator |
| Internal Model Principle | What dynamics must be internalized for robust regulation? | internal model of reference/disturbance dynamics | persistent PCC cycling may require a dynamical internal model | compare controllers with/without cycle model |

## Layered interpretation

A useful working decomposition is:

- **Ashby:** required regulatory capacity.
- **Good Regulator:** required model adequacy.
- **PCC:** candidate description of the instability-generating interaction structure.
- **EBID:** candidate measurement of the resulting instability.

These are treated as potentially complementary layers, not competing theories.
